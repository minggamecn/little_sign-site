ACCOUNT & DATA

Request deletion of your Little Sign account

You can request deletion here by email without installing the app. Deletion is permanent.

Fastest route: in the app, open Settings → Delete my account. After email verification the account is deleted immediately.

What deletion means

Your account, sign-in sessions and account-linked service records are permanently deleted.

Cards and notes are stored on your devices. Deletion cannot erase those local copies. Use “Clear my data on this device” in Little Sign on each device to remove them.

How to request deletion by email

  1. Send an email from the address you use to sign in to Little Sign to ming.life@foxmail.com with the subject “Delete my Little Sign account”.
  2. We verify ownership by the sending address and may reply to confirm the request. We never ask for a verification code or password.
  3. We delete the account and its account-linked service records within 5 days of receiving a verifiable request, then reply to confirm. This cannot be undone.

Open my email app with the request

If the button does not open an email app, copy this message:

Delete my Little Sign account

Please permanently delete my Little Sign account.
Account email: (the address you sign in with — send this message from that address)

If you can no longer send from the account address, explain this in your message. We need to establish ownership and cannot delete an account based only on an address someone else supplies.
