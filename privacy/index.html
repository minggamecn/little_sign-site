Privacy policy

2. Information on your device

Daily cards, notes, intentions, saved readings, decisions and their status, wheel options, mood and usage signals, themes, and language preferences are stored on your device. A local installation identifier helps keep draws consistent; it is not an advertising ID. Core guest features do not require an account.

These local records do not automatically sync to our servers. The current release has no cloud journal backup. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs to upload these records. Signing out does not erase device data.

3. Email accounts and service records

When you register or sign in, Supabase and the verification-email delivery service process your email address, code verification, account identifier, and sign-in sessions to verify identity and provide account features. Sign-in sessions are stored on your device; verification codes are not saved in your journal.

We keep an account-linked AI request date and count on the server to enforce usage limits and control abuse. Those counters do not contain your question, answer, or notes.

4. Optional AI questions

When you explicitly submit a custom AI question, your question, optional context, and drawn-card information pass through our Supabase service to DeepSeek to generate a reading. Your text may include personal information you choose to enter. Avoid sensitive information you do not want processed and other people’s private information.

We do not include your email, account ID, private notes, history, or mood signals in the AI prompt. Your account still authenticates the request to our service. The answer returns to the app and is written to local history only when you choose to save it. The app backend does not store questions and answers in a journal database or deliberately log question bodies. Provider operational logging, retention, and processing can be separate from this flow.

We do not use your local journal to train AI. This policy does not promise zero retention or no training by the AI provider for submitted text; those conditions depend on the applicable API agreement and configured settings. You can avoid AI processing and continue using offline cards and authored scenarios.

5. Sharing, photos, and device permissions

When you choose to export or share a card, the app creates an image and passes it to the system sharing app you select, or saves it to your photo library. Current card sharing excludes private notes, intentions, and custom questions. Recipients and the apps you choose handle the shared image under their own rules.

Saving an image may require storage permission on older Android versions or permission to add photos on iOS. The app does not need to read your entire photo library, and its core features do not collect contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone content. Manage permissions in your device settings.

6. Providers, website, and network information

Supabase provides authentication, the database, and the app backend. Email delivery services send verification codes. DeepSeek processes submitted AI questions. GitHub Pages hosts this website. Network providers receive information such as your IP address needed to handle connections, and may process request times, browser or device information, and security logs to run, secure, and diagnose services.

The website sets no cookies of its own and stores nothing in your browser; the deletion request page only opens your email app with a prefilled message and sends nothing itself. The website adds no advertising or marketing tracking. The hosting platform may use its own security or access-control cookies. If you email us, email services process your address and the content you provide.

We do not sell personal information. We do not deliberately disclose your records except to providers delivering the service, recipients you choose when sharing, or where necessary for legal requirements or service security. Processing may occur outside your country. Provider information: Supabase · DeepSeek · GitHub.

A provider’s policy for its own users may not cover data submitted through an API; this Little Sign policy and the applicable service agreements govern the corresponding processing.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local records remain until you delete them or use Settings → Clear my data on this device. Uninstalling, losing, or resetting a device can also cause data loss; exported images and other copies must be deleted separately.
  • Account records remain until you delete the account. Use Settings → Delete my account in the app, or email a request as described on the account deletion page. In-app deletion runs immediately after verification and confirmation; an email request is verified by the sending address and completed within 5 days of a verifiable request, with a confirmation reply. Both remove the account, linked sign-in records, per-account usage counts, and other linked records covered by the deployed data relationships. Signing out or clearing this device does not delete the account.
  • Deleting an account from the app removes identifiable AI history belonging to that account on that device. Guest records, older unowned records, and other accounts’ records remain. We cannot erase local copies on any device; clear data on each device separately.
  • Usage counts reset on a request on a new day; successful request processing removes counts older than two calendar days. Cleanup is request-triggered, not a timed guarantee. Account deletion removes its count; the global count without account identifiers remains.
  • Provider logs, backups, and submitted AI text may remain under applicable agreements for security, abuse prevention, service operation, or legal obligations. Immediate account deletion does not guarantee erasure of those copies. Contact us below for the retention periods applicable to your request or deletion assistance. We create no new server account record to track deletion.

8. Security, choices, and rights

Account and AI requests use HTTPS. The backend verifies account authorization, and administrative keys are not included in the app or website. Local storage relies on device and app-sandbox protection; it is not a separately encrypted journal vault. Protect your device and email account; no system guarantees absolute security.

You can use guest mode, avoid submitting AI questions, sign out, manage permissions, delete local records, or request account deletion. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, receive a copy of, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, withdraw consent, and complain to a regulator. We may need to verify identity; we will not ask you to send a verification code or password.

For a rights request or assistance: ming.life@foxmail.com. Support.

9. Children and policy updates

Little Sign offers everyday reflection and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact the operator so we can investigate and handle it under applicable law.

This page shows its update date. Material changes to data handling will be communicated through the app or another appropriate channel; consent will be requested separately where required.
