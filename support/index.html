Contact Little Sign

For app problems, privacy requests, or an inappropriate reading, contact: ming.life@foxmail.com.

Include the app version, device operating system, when the problem happened, and steps to reproduce it. Share only what is needed to resolve the issue. Remove private notes and email addresses from screenshots; never send verification codes, passwords, or someone else’s private information.

Missing codes or sign-in trouble

Check the email spelling and spam folder, wait for the resend countdown, and use the newest code. Signing in does not automatically register an account; new users should explicitly choose Create an account in the app.

If the network or email service is unavailable, return to guest mode for offline features. You do not need to send us your code.

Delete an account or device data

To permanently delete an account, choose Delete my account in app Settings, or follow the account deletion page and email the request from your account address. In-app deletion requires identity verification and confirmation; an email request is verified by the sending address and completed within 5 days, with a confirmation reply. Clear my data on this device is a separate action: it removes this device’s records and signs you out, but leaves the account intact.

If the in-app connection drops after deletion was sent, use Check and retry deletion; lack of a response does not prove failure. If you cannot access your email, contact support; we need to establish ownership and cannot delete someone’s information based only on an account address.

Offline content and saved records

Daily cards and authored scenarios work offline. Generating a new AI reading requires connectivity and a verified account. Saved AI readings stay on the device where they were saved; signing out hides account-owned entries, and signing back into the same account restores access. This release has no cross-device journal sync.

Report an inappropriate AI reading

You can reject or avoid saving any reading. Use the support email above to describe the concern, when it happened, and any relevant excerpt you choose to share. Reports can contain sensitive information; include only what is needed and tell us if you want the supplied report content deleted.

The support inbox is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
