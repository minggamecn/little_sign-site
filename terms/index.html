6. Sparks, payments, and third-party services

Readings written by Little Sign cost sparks. Every verified account receives 10 sparks once, and a completed Today draw earns 1 spark per day while you are signed in and online. A reading costs 5 sparks, or 10 for the ten-card Celtic Cross; the app shows the price and your balance before you confirm. A reading that fails, is declined, or asks you to rephrase costs nothing. Cancelling the wait in the app does not always stop a reading that our service has already started: a reading that completes is charged even if you did not stay to read it. If you were charged and received no reading, contact support.

Where Google Play offers it, you can buy a pack of 50 sparks. Google Play sets and shows the price in your local currency before you confirm, processes the payment, and handles refunds under its own policies. Sparks have no cash value, cannot be transferred, cashed out, or used outside Little Sign, and do not expire. A refund or reversal of a pack removes all 50 sparks, even if some were used, so a balance can fall below zero until you add sparks again. There is no subscription in this release. Your network provider may charge for data use.

Email, sharing, and other third-party services you choose are operated separately. Their terms may apply when you use them.
